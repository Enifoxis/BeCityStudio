Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how BeCityStudio ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal data by default. The following may be stored locally in your browser:

  • Theme preference (dark / light mode)
  • Language preference
  • Music and volume settings

These settings are stored only in your browser's localStorage and are never sent to our servers.

3. Cookies

This website currently does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Only essential local storage is used for user preferences.

4. Third-Party Services

We load Font Awesome icons from a CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com). No personal data is shared with this service by us.

5. Your Rights

You can clear all stored preferences at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the channels listed on the Social page or in the Imprint.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent version.
